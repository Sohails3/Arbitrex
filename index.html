The product

Dense data, calmly presented

Financial data is unavoidably dense. The interface should not be. Arbitrex keeps information density low and the signal high — generous whitespace, soft elevation, and one accent colour reserved for what genuinely matters.

Screening

Filter the register on the criteria that actually define your mandate, then work the results without leaving the page.

Company detail

Filed accounts, officers and history in a single drawer — opened beside your results rather than replacing them.

Mandate fit

A single score that says how closely a company matches your criteria, with the inputs behind it always visible.

Lists

Build and share working lists of companies, kept current as new filings arrive rather than frozen at export.

Map

See geographic concentration across a portfolio or pipeline at a glance, filtered by the same criteria as your screen.

Watch

A trigger inbox that tells you when something you're tracking changes — new filing, new officer, new signal.

Provenance

An estimate never looks like a fact

Every value carries a label naming where it came from. Modelled figures get both an amber tag and a dashed underline — so you always know what you are relying on before you act on it.
