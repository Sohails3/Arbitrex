Combined research

Our approach

Research first.
Conviction second.

Every strategy we run begins as a hypothesis and survives only if it holds up against out-of-sample data, realistic transaction costs, and adversarial review. Most do not. That filter is the product.

Signal research

We mine cross-asset datasets for durable, economically-motivated edges — then subject each candidate to out-of-sample testing before it earns capital.

Risk management

Position limits, drawdown controls and correlation monitoring are enforced in code, not convention. Every strategy runs inside a hard risk envelope.

Execution infrastructure

Co-located, latency-optimised order routing with continuous slippage attribution — because a strategy is only as good as its fills.

Portfolio construction

Capital is allocated across uncorrelated strategies, sized by contribution to portfolio risk rather than standalone historical return.

Transparent reporting

Clear attribution of returns, costs and risk exposures. No black boxes, no unexplained variance, no surprises in the monthly letter.

Global coverage

Strategies operate across equities, futures and FX in developed and liquid emerging markets, around the clock through the trading week.

How we work

From hypothesis to live capital

01 — RESEARCH

Form the hypothesis

An edge has to make economic sense before it makes money. We start with a causal story, then look for it in the data — never the reverse.

02 — VALIDATE

Try to break it

Out-of-sample windows, realistic cost assumptions, regime stress tests and independent review. Strategies that survive are rare and deliberately so.

03 — DEPLOY

Scale under constraint

Live capital starts small and grows only as realised behaviour matches expectation. Risk limits are enforced automatically from day one.
